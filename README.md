# 🏠 Airbnb Analytics: Global Performance Dashboard

A dynamic and visually driven Power BI dashboard built to analyze Airbnb’s global growth, customer engagement, pricing trends, trust indicators, and market performance across major cities worldwide.

The dashboard transforms raw Airbnb listing and review data into actionable business insights using storytelling visuals, KPI tracking, and analytical comparisons.

---

# 📌 Short Description / Purpose

The Airbnb Global Performance Dashboard is an interactive Power BI report designed to explore Airbnb’s worldwide ecosystem through listings, reviews, pricing behavior, customer trust, and regional performance trends.

This dashboard helps:
- 📊 Data Analysts
- 🌍 Tourism Strategists
- 🏢 Business Stakeholders
- 📈 Market Researchers

understand Airbnb’s platform evolution and identify key operational insights.

---

# 🛠️ Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:

- 📊 Power BI Desktop – Main data visualization platform used for report creation
- 📂 Power Query – Data transformation and cleaning layer
- 🧠 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Used for KPIs, measures, and dynamic calculations
- 🔗 Data Modeling – Relationships established among multiple datasets
- 📈 Analytical Storytelling – Trend analysis, heatmaps, Pareto charts, and comparative visuals
- 📁 File Formats – `.pbix` for development and `.png` for dashboard previews

# 📊 Walkthrough of Key Visuals

---

## 🔹 KPI Overview Cards

Top-level KPIs summarizing platform scale:

- 🏠 Total Listings: **279K+**
- 🌍 Cities Covered: **10**
- 👤 Hosts: **182K+**
- 🏡 Property Types: **144**
- ⭐ Reviews: **5.3M+**

---

## 📈 New Listings Growth Analysis

Lifecycle visualization showing Airbnb’s market evolution phases:

- Introduction
- Growth
- Maturity
- Decline
- Reinvention
- COVID-19 Impact

### Key Insights:
- Peak growth occurred around **2015**
- Regulations slowed expansion after 2016
- COVID-19 caused major listing decline

---

## 📝 Review Frequency Analysis

Pareto-style chart analyzing reviewer activity.

### Insights:
- Most users leave only **1 review**
- Nearly **98%** of users contribute fewer than 5 reviews
- Very few users behave as review outliers

---

## 🛡️ Trust & Verification Analysis

Verification analysis comparing trusted vs. unverified hosts.

### Insights:
- Over **66%** of hosts are verified
- Unverified profiles are minimal
- Verification significantly improves marketplace trust

---

## 🌍 Seasonality Heatmap

Monthly review activity comparison across major cities.

### Findings:
- Paris & Rome dominate during summer
- New York peaks during holiday seasons
- Seasonal trends differ by region

---

## 🏙️ Market Share by City

City-wise listing and review contribution analysis.

### Insights:
- Paris, NYC, and Sydney dominate the platform
- Market concentration favors tourism-heavy cities
- Listings and reviews strongly correlate

---

## 💰 Pricing Analysis

Room-type pricing comparison:

- Hotel Rooms
- Entire Places
- Shared Rooms
- Private Rooms

### Key Findings:
- Hotel rooms are most expensive
- Shared rooms are most affordable
- Entire places dominate customer demand

---

## ⭐ Ratings Analysis

Comparative city ratings visualization.

### Insights:
- Mexico City & Rio scored highest
- Hong Kong & Istanbul showed lower satisfaction
- Communication ratings performed best overall
